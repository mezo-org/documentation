Each MEZO Gauge receives a share of the non-staking gauge emissions equal to its share of the veMEZO votes cast on MEZO Gauges that epoch.

If 1,000,000 veMEZO votes across all MEZO Gauges and 300,000 of them go to one gauge, that gauge takes 30% of the epoch's directable MEZO. The rest of this page uses USDC/MUSD as an example. The same split applies to every MEZO Gauge.

---

## Curve and Uniswap via Merkl

Curve USDT/USDC/MUSD and Uniswap USDC/MUSD are incentivised through [Merkl](https://merkl.xyz), the same mechanism Mezo already uses for these pools, now each with a MEZO Gauge.

<Steps>
1. **Epoch N closes.** Votes are tallied and each gauge's MEZO allocation is fixed.
2. **The allocation is posted to Merkl** as a campaign on the destination pool.
3. **LPs accrue a claim** proportional to their share of the pool over the campaign period. Claims occur on Merkl.
</Steps>

**Rewards arrive as veMEZO locked for 3 months**, not as liquid MEZO. This matches how these pools are incentivised today, but now voters decide how much emissions each pool gets.

See [Claiming Incentives](/docs/users/mezo-earn/vote/mezo-gauges/claiming-incentives) for the LP-side walkthrough.

## MEZO as incentives on Aero

Aero pools work differently, because Aero runs its own reward system. sAERO holders allocate to pools, and those allocations direct **AERO Rewards** to each pool's AERO-earning deposits. sAERO replaced veAERO when Aerodrome became Aero.

Take USDC/MUSD as an example. MEZO does not go to Aero LPs directly. It is posted on that pool as an **incentive program**, and its payments, which Aero calls Liquidity Payments, go to the sAERO holders who allocate to the pool.

Those allocations are what direct AERO Rewards to the pool. The MEZO buys AERO Rewards for the pool, and it is the AERO that reaches LPs. The same path applies to MEZO/MUSD on Aero.

Mechanically, the MEZO is claimed on Mezo, bridged to Base, and posted on the pool by an allocator contract. This runs automatically each epoch.

:::caution[Reconnecting after the Aero upgrade]
Aerodrome's old gauges shut down when Aero launched on October 22, 2026. Mezo is connecting the allocator to the new Aero gauges. Until that's done, MEZO directed to the Base gauges stays on Mezo and is not bridged.
:::

### Timing on Aero

Aero has no weekly vote. sAERO holders allocate continuously, and a change takes effect as soon as it's made. Each sAERO position can change its allocation once every 47 hours, Aero's launch setting. Incentives stream to the sAERO holders allocating to the pool, and AERO Rewards stream to the pool's AERO-earning deposits. Neither waits for an epoch flip.

The weekly step is on the Mezo side:

| Epoch | What happens |
|---|---|
| **N** | You vote on Mezo. The epoch closes and the allocation is fixed. |
| **N+1** | The MEZO is bridged to Base and posted on the pool as a one-week incentive program, by the **Distribution date** shown on the Vote page. |
| **The following week** | The program streams the MEZO to the sAERO holders allocating to the pool. |

MEZO that streams while no sAERO is allocated to the pool isn't paid out. The allocator can recover it after the program ends and post it again in a later program.

## Capturing the value you direct

Directing emissions is influence, not yield. The MEZO you send to a destination pool goes to the people on that pool — Merkl claimants on Curve and Uniswap, sAERO holders on Aero. It does not come back to you as a MEZO Gauge voter.

What you earn directly as a voter is your share of **incentives posted on the MEZO Gauge itself**, in proportion to your share of that gauge's votes.

To capture the emissions themselves, you have to be on both sides of the loop. On Aero, using USDC/MUSD as an example:

<Steps>
1. **LP** into the pool on Aero, and choose **AERO rewards** for the deposit.
2. **Vote** for that pool's MEZO Gauge on Mezo, with veMEZO.
3. **Allocate** sAERO to the same pool on Aero.
4. **Collect** AERO Rewards for the LP position, and the MEZO incentive for the sAERO allocation.
</Steps>

On Curve and Uniswap the loop is shorter: LP into the pool, vote for its MEZO Gauge, and claim the campaign on Merkl as an LP.

:::note
Running the full loop is optional. Plenty of veMEZO holders will vote purely to strengthen MUSD liquidity and to earn incentives posted on the gauge.
:::
