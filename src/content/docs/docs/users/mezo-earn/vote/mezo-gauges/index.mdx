**Mezo Gauges let veMEZO holders vote to direct MEZO emissions to MUSD liquidity pools outside Mezo** — on Curve and Uniswap (Ethereum) and Aerodrome (Base).

Until now, veMEZO had one job: [boosting](/docs/users/mezo-earn/lock/vemezo/boost-mechanism) a veBTC position. Mezo Gauges give it a second one. veMEZO now carries voting power of its own, over a real slice of weekly emissions.

They also change who decides. Incentives for MUSD liquidity off Mezo used to be a Foundation allocation. They are now a weekly vote.

---

## Why pay LPs on other chains?

This is the obvious question, so it is worth answering directly.

MUSD is only useful if it is liquid where people already are. Thin pools off Mezo mean MUSD cannot be entered or exited at size, and that caps how large the MUSD debt book can safely grow — and the debt book is the product. Paying for MUSD depth on Curve, Uniswap, and Aerodrome buys MUSD utility; it does not subsidise a competitor.

The liquidity was already being paid for. What changes is that veMEZO holders now decide where it goes.

## How Mezo Gauges differ from other gauges

Mezo operates three kinds of gauge. It is worth being clear on which is which.

| | Staking gauge | veBTC boost gauge | **Mezo Gauge** |
|---|---|---|---|
| You vote with | veBTC | veMEZO | **veMEZO** |
| Anyone stakes LP tokens? | Yes | No | **No** |
| Emissions land | On Mezo, to stakers | On Mezo, as boost | **Off Mezo, as pool incentives** |
| Voter earns | Trading fees + incentives | Posted incentives | **Incentives posted on the gauge** |

Mezo Gauges are **non-staking gauges**: there is no LP token to stake on Mezo, because the liquidity being incentivised lives on another chain. Voting on them is entirely independent of your veBTC voting and of boost matching. Directing veMEZO to a Mezo Gauge does not consume or reduce any boost you are providing.

:::note
"Non-staking gauge" is the general mechanism. Mezo Gauges are the first ones live, and every launch gauge is a *remote* gauge — it routes emissions to another chain. The mechanism can support non-staking gauges that stay on Mezo in future.
:::

## Which pools are eligible

Three gauges are live at launch, all of them MUSD stable pools:

| Gauge | Pool | Venue | Chain | Distribution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uniswap USDC/MUSD | USDC/MUSD | Uniswap | Ethereum | Merkl |
| Curve USDT/USDC/MUSD | USDT/USDC/MUSD | Curve | Ethereum | Merkl |
| Aerodrome USDC/MUSD | USDC/MUSD | Aerodrome | Base | Aerodrome voting incentives |

Curve and Uniswap share a distribution path but are **separate gauges** — you vote on them independently.

### How the list changes

The set of eligible pools is an on-chain allowlist held by the remote gauge registry and controlled by governance. Pools are added or removed by governance action, not by anyone permissionlessly creating a gauge. When the list changes, it changes here and on the Vote page.

## How much MEZO is directable

Mezo Gauges are funded from the **non-staking gauge branch** of emissions — currently **8% of weekly emission rewards**, a share that veBTC holders set through the [splitter](/docs/users/mezo-earn/overview/glossary#splitter).

At current emission levels that is roughly **250,000 MEZO per epoch**, with a further **~300,000 MEZO per epoch** in supplemental rewards.

This does not come out of what veBTC holders receive from staking gauges. It is a separate branch of the same emissions tree, and its size is itself a veBTC vote. See [Emissions Schedule](/docs/users/mezo/emissions) for the full flow.

## The loop, end to end

<Steps>
1. **You vote.** Direct veMEZO voting power across the available Mezo Gauges during the epoch.
2. **The epoch closes** Thursday 00:00 UTC and votes are tallied. Each gauge's share of MEZO is set by its share of veMEZO votes.
3. **MEZO leaves Mezo.** Each gauge's allocation is claimed and routed to its destination — bridged to Base for Aerodrome, or handed to Merkl for Curve and Uniswap.
4. **It lands as an incentive** on the destination pool.
5. **LPs claim it** on Merkl or Aerodrome.
</Steps>

The exact timing and form differ by venue. See [Where Your Vote Goes](/docs/users/mezo-earn/vote/mezo-gauges/where-your-vote-goes).

---

## What a voter earns

Voting on a Mezo Gauge earns you **a share of any incentives posted on that gauge**, proportional to your share of that gauge's veMEZO votes — the same way [boost gauge](/docs/users/mezo-earn/lock/vemezo/matching-market) incentives work.

Be clear about what it does *not* do: the MEZO you direct to an Aerodrome or Merkl pool goes to **that pool's LPs and voters**, not back to you. Directing emissions is influence over where MUSD liquidity gets paid for, not a yield on its own.

If you want to capture the value you direct, you have to be on the other side of it too — LP into the destination pool, and on Aerodrome, vote for the corresponding gauge on Base. [Where Your Vote Goes](/docs/users/mezo-earn/vote/mezo-gauges/where-your-vote-goes#capturing-the-value-you-direct) sets out that loop.
