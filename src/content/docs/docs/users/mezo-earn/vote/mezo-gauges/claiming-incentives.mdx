Who claims what, and where, depends on which side of the gauge you are on.

| You are | You claim | Where |
|---|---|---|
| An LP in Curve USDT/USDC/MUSD or Uniswap USDC/MUSD | veMEZO locked for 3 months | [Merkl](https://app.merkl.xyz) |
| An sAERO holder allocating to Aero USDC/MUSD or MEZO/MUSD | MEZO, as an incentive | [Aero](https://app.aero.xyz) |
| An LP in Aero USDC/MUSD or MEZO/MUSD | AERO Rewards | [Aero](https://app.aero.xyz) |
| A veMEZO holder who voted on a MEZO Gauge | Incentives posted on that gauge | The Vote tab on [mezo.org](https://mezo.org) |

## Curve and Uniswap LPs: Claim on Merkl

Rewards are **not** pushed to your wallet. Merkl accrues a claim against your address, and you have to collect it.

<Steps>
1. Go to [app.merkl.xyz](https://app.merkl.xyz).
2. Connect the wallet holding the LP position.
3. Open **Dashboard**. After you connect, it lists every reward that wallet is eligible to claim, so you do not need to search for the campaign.
4. Click **Claim** and sign the transaction.
</Steps>

**What you receive:** veMEZO locked for 3 months, not liquid MEZO. Once claimed, it behaves like any other [veMEZO position](/docs/users/mezo-earn/lock/vemezo). It can boost a veBTC position, and it can vote on MEZO Gauges.

If you LP in the pool, you can loop this by voting the claimed veMEZO back onto that pool's MEZO Gauge, which sends more emissions to the position you already hold. Optional. Providing liquidity during the campaign is enough to claim. See [Where Your Vote Goes](/docs/users/mezo-earn/vote/mezo-gauges/where-your-vote-goes#capturing-the-value-you-direct) for the full path.

:::caution[Unclaimed rewards are forfeited]
Merkl campaigns do not accrue indefinitely. If you never claim, you lose the rewards. If you LP in these pools, check Merkl periodically.
:::

You do not need to hold veMEZO, or have voted, to claim as an LP. Providing liquidity to the pool during the campaign is the only requirement.

## Aero: Claim on Base

MEZO directed to an Aero gauge is deposited as an **incentive**, so it is earned by sAERO holders who allocate to the pool, not by LPs.

**If you hold sAERO and allocate to USDC/MUSD or MEZO/MUSD:** incentives stream to you while you stay allocated, and you can claim them at any time on [app.aero.xyz](https://app.aero.xyz). Your MEZO share is proportional to your share of the allocation on that pool.

**If you LP in Aero USDC/MUSD or MEZO/MUSD:** you earn **AERO Rewards**, which stream continuously to the pool's LPs. See [Timing on Aero](/docs/users/mezo-earn/vote/mezo-gauges/where-your-vote-goes#timing-on-aero) for how a Mezo vote reaches the pool.

### Moving from Aerodrome

Aerodrome emissions stopped when Aero launched on October 22, 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Positions do not move on their own.

- **LPs in the old USDC/MUSD or MEZO/MUSD pool on Base:** migrate your position to the new Aero pool through the Aero Upgrade Portal. Follow [Aero's LP guidance](https://aero.xyz/aero-upgrade/#liquidity-providers).
- **veAERO holders:** veAERO was replaced by sAERO. Upgrading resets your old votes, so allocate the new sAERO position to the Aero USDC/MUSD or MEZO/MUSD pool to keep earning MEZO incentives. See [Aero's holder guidance](https://aero.xyz/aero-upgrade/#veaero-vevelo-holders).

## MEZO Gauge voters: Claim on Mezo

If you directed veMEZO to a MEZO Gauge, you earn a share of any **incentives posted on that gauge**, proportional to your share of its votes.

Claim these the same way as any other voting reward — on the **Vote** tab at [mezo.org](https://mezo.org), once the epoch has closed. See [Claiming Fees & Emissions](/docs/users/mezo-earn/vote/claiming-fees-emissions).

These incentives are separate from the emissions you directed. The emissions go to the destination pool; the incentives are posted on Mezo to attract your vote there in the first place.
