## BTC Vault wind-down

The BTC Vault (mbhBTC), operated with Mellow and Sense Capital, is winding down. Deposits are closed. Withdraw your funds by **October 28, 2026**.

Withdrawals are queued, so start early. You must request your withdrawal by October 28; processing and claiming can happen afterward. Requesting a withdrawal does not immediately return your funds to your wallet.

### Withdrawing from the BTC Vault

1. Open the [BTC Vault](https://mezo.org/earn/vaults/0xE2232789D4cF5bb1ffaDA1a105Cc59B18d639318) and connect the wallet that holds your position.
2. Select **Withdraw**, choose the amount and follow the transaction prompts.
3. Wait for your withdrawal request to be processed.
4. Return to the vault page and claim your tokens when they are ready.

If you previously migrated cbBTC Vault shares into the BTC Vault, these instructions apply to that position too. There is no replacement vault as part of this wind-down.

### Finding your position

Once the wind-down is announced, the BTC Vault appears in the vault list only for wallets with a remaining position. Holders continue to see it after October 28, including while a withdrawal is pending or ready to claim. You can also open the vault directly using the link above.

### Help after October 28

Contact [Mellow support on Discord](https://discord.com/invite/mellow) for help withdrawing after October 28.

## cbBTC Vault withdrawals

Deposits to the cbBTC Vault are closed, and migration to the BTC Vault has ended. If you still hold cbBTC Vault shares (mbhcbBTC), withdraw your remaining funds from the cbBTC Vault. Withdrawals remain available in the app.

Open [Mezo Vaults](https://mezo.org/earn/vaults), connect the wallet that holds your cbBTC Vault position and select **Withdraw** on the cbBTC Vault page.

## Stablecoin Vault wind-down

The Stablecoin Vault, operated by Lhava on Mellow's vault framework, is winding down. Deposits are already paused.

If you have a position in the vault, start your withdrawal by **July 31, 2026**. The vault supports positions funded with mUSDC, mUSDT, or MUSD and issues **msvUSD** receipt tokens. Withdrawals are processed in mUSDC, regardless of whether your position was funded with mUSDC, mUSDT, or MUSD.

### How to withdraw

1. Go to [Mezo Vaults](https://mezo.org/earn/vaults) and open the **Stablecoin Vault**.
2. Select **Withdraw** and follow the prompts for your position.
3. Confirm the transaction and allow approximately 48 hours for the withdrawal queue to process.

After July 31, 2026, the Stablecoin Vault will no longer appear in the app. Mellow will manage the remaining funds and process late withdrawals. If you miss the withdrawal window, [open a support ticket in Mellow's Discord](https://discord.com/invite/mellow).
