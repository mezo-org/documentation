## Platform Fundamentals

### What is Lolli?

Lolli is a Bitcoin rewards platform that allows users to earn Bitcoin (measured in satoshis or "sats") and cashback when shopping at over x stores across x+ retail partners. Users can earn rewards through the browser extension, mobile app, or website by making purchases or playing mobile games. Lolli has rewarded over x users with $x+ million in Bitcoin since its launch in 2018.

### What is Mezo?

Mezo is a Bitcoin bank built by Thesis that lets you use your Bitcoin for everyday finance without ever selling it. On Mezo, Bitcoin holders can borrow money, earn yield, and spend — all powered by MUSD, a Bitcoin-backed stablecoin. It's designed so you can access your Bitcoin's value while keeping your Bitcoin. Visit mezo.

### Where does each platform operate?

- **Lolli**: Currently available in the United States. Global expansion is planned.
- **Mezo**: Operates as a blockchain platform accessible globally to anyone with a Bitcoin & EVM wallet.

## Earning Mechanisms

### What are sats?

Satoshis (sats) are the smallest unit of Bitcoin. One Bitcoin = 100,000,000 satoshis. When you earn on Lolli, you receive actual Bitcoin denominated in sats. These are real BTC that you own and can transfer to your own wallet or withdraw.

### How can I earn sats on Lolli?

1. Daily Stack: Participate in daily challenges for a chance to win up to $X+ daily
2. Playing Games: Complete games in the Lolli Arcade and earn rewards

More ways to earn sats coming soon.

### What are mats?

Mats are Mezo's reward points system. You earn them for shopping, playing games, borrowing, depositing Bitcoin, and engaging with the community. They have no current monetary value but unlock access to features and opportunities within the broader Bitcoin ecosystem. Think of mats as Bitcoin-friendly rewards points. For more info, check here

With the Lolli x Mezo partnership, alongside collecting mats on Mezo, Lolli users will be able to collect mats with their actions on Lolli.

### How can I earn mats on Lolli?

On webapp:

- 3 Cups Game: It's a daily chance for you play, have fun and get extra rewards — mats

On mobile coming soon! With the Lolli x Mezo partnership, you will earn mats whenever you earn sats:

1. Daily Stack: Participate in daily challenges for a chance to win up to X+ mats daily
2. Playing Games: Complete games in the Lolli Arcade and earn rewards

### How can I earn mats on Mezo?

1. Vault Deposits: Deposit assets into the MUSD Vault to earn mats based on deposit size and duration.
2. Providing Liquidity to Mezo Pools: Supply tokens to trading pairs on Mezo Pools to earn mats from liquidity provision.

## Withdrawal & Conversion

### How can I withdraw my sats from Lolli?

Lolli withdrawals are reopening through Mezo, starting with Bitcoin (sats). You’ll need to connect or create a Mezo account to withdraw.

USD withdrawals (as MUSD on Mezo) are coming next, and we’re also working on adding more withdrawal destinations in the future to give you more flexibility.

### How can I withdraw my sats with Mezo?

You can withdraw $30 worth of sats or more to Mezo. Follow the steps below:

1. In your Lolli account select “Withdraw”: https://www.lolli.com/wallet
2. You’ll be prompted to connect your Mezo account and confirm account linking details
   - Need a Mezo account? Head to Mezo.org to sign up
   - Only a web3 wallet address (Bitcoin or Ethereum) is required
3. Once linked proceed in Lolli with withdraw to specify amount of BTC (SATS) you would like transferred to your Mezo wallet
   - Minimum $30 withdrawal threshold
   - USD → MUSD & BTC → MUSD withdrawals coming soon
4. Funds will appear in your Mezo account within ~10 minutes
5. Once funds available on Mezo they are yours to transact as you please
   - Hold funds as BTC on Mezo
   - Utilize DeFi products on Mezo like vaults to earn yield (e.g. MUSD Vault)
   - Use the “Swap” feature to convert your BTC to other assets on Mezo like MUSD (e.g. for items like giftcards in the MUSD Market)

### Why is $30 needed as minimum amount to withdraw to Mezo?

You can withdraw $30 worth of sats or more to Mezo. This is to ensure you can buy a Bitrefill gift card on the Mezo Market for at least $25 and have enough sats to pay for transactions.

### How can I convert mats to sats?

Mats and sats are two different types of rewards with different purposes, so they don't convert to each other. Sats are real Bitcoin that you earn on Lolli and own immediately with monetary value. Mats are loyalty points you earn on both Lolli and Mezo that give you access to the broader Bitcoin ecosystem. You earn both simultaneously, but they serve different functions — sats build your Bitcoin wealth, while mats unlock ecosystem features and benefits.

## Integration & Connection

### Do I have to connect to Mezo to use Lolli?

No, Lolli works independently. However, connecting to Mezo is optional and recommended because it provides:

- Faster withdrawals (replacing the old 60-90 day wait)
- Ability to earn yield on your sats rewards
- Claiming your mats rewards
- Access to Mezo's borrowing features (take loans against your BTC at 1% fixed rate)
- Integration with a broader Bitcoin financial ecosystem

### Do I have to have a wallet to use Mezo?

Yes, you need a Bitcoin or EVM wallet to use Mezo. Recommended wallets include:

- Bitcoin wallets: Unisat, OKX, Xverse
- EVM wallets: Taho, Metamask, Zerion, OKX

You don't necessarily need to connect to Mezo to use Lolli, but you'll need a wallet to receive your mats on Mezo.

### What happens to my sats on Lolli?

Your sats are held in your Lolli account. You can:

1. Withdraw to your own wallet (once you hit the $X minimum)
2. Transfer to Mezo for yield-earning strategies
3. Hold and continue earning more sats through your actions

### How can I utilize my sats on Mezo?

Once transferred to Mezo, your Bitcoin (in sats) can be:

1. Used as Collateral: Borrow MUSD (Bitcoin-backed stablecoins) at 1% fixed rate, you need min amount
2. Earn Yield: Deposit into MUSD vaults for passive income
3. Trade: Use Swaps to trade or provide liquidity
4. Spend: Use MUSD for real-world purchases via partners (BitRefill, gift cards, etc.)
5. Hold & Earn: Simply lock your BTC on Mezo to earn mats rewards daily

### How can I utilize my mats on Mezo?

On Mezo Discord:

- Tip other community members on discord
- Participate in games on discord
- Complete community bounties on discord
- Access exclusive features and leaderboards
- Access to exclusive features or financial opportunities in bitcoin ecosystem

## Circular Bitcoin Economy

### What's the connection between Lolli and Mezo?

Lolli and Mezo are both owned by Thesis (a venture studio), creating an integrated Bitcoin ecosystem:

- Earn Path: Take action on Lolli → Earn sats and mats
- Reward Path:
  - Transfer sats to Mezo → Access finance features
  - Claim mats on Mezo → Access opportunities in wider Bitcoin ecosystem
- Circular Path: Use Mezo's MUSD to spend or earn yield → Reinvest earnings

### How does the partnership unlock value?

- Speed: Instant or fast withdrawals replace 60-90 day waits
- Flexibility: Multiple withdrawal options
- Growth: Earned sats can accelerate start earning yield on Mezo
- Utility: Bitcoin becomes spendable and usable, not just stored
- Ecosystem: Access to integrated DeFi features (borrowing, yields, swaps)

### What is Mezo's MUSD stablecoin?

MUSD is a permissionless stablecoin 100% backed by Bitcoin reserves and designed to maintain a 1:1 peg with the U.S. dollar. It is the native stablecoin on Mezo, accessible via Mezo’s ‘Borrow’ feature or decentralized exchanges on Mezo Network. Check here for more info
